Our Story

Drayer Physical Therapy was founded by a team of therapists who wanted to make rehabilitation more personal and accessible. After seeing how many patients struggled to recover from injuries and surgeries, they created a clinic focused on compassionate care and individualized treatment plans.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral?
No, many patients can schedule directly with us.

What should I bring to my first appointment?
Bring a photo ID, insurance card, and any medical records related to your condition.

How long are therapy sessions?
Most sessions last between 45 and 60 minutes.
